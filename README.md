# TiffinWale - All-India Tiffin Service Discovery Platform

A modern, fast, mobile-first local search and lead-generation platform for discovering reliable homemade tiffin services across India.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Deployment
- Built with standard HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), Bootstrap 5.3, and Bootstrap Icons.
- 100% compatible with **GitHub Pages** (zero backend / zero build step required).

## 🍽️ Key Features Built for the Homepage
1. **Sticky Header & Brand Identity**: Clean navigation with mobile offcanvas drawer and prominent "List Your Tiffin Service" CTA.
2. **Hero Search & Geolocation**:
   - Location search by City, Area, or PIN code with live input validation.
   - Click-activated browser Geolocation (`Use My Location`) with error fallback.
   - Interactive service preference chips (Veg, Jain, Non-Veg, Monthly, Daily, Lunch, Dinner).
   - High-tech SVG culinary thali artwork with trust badges.
3. **Compact Trust Signals**: Verified kitchens, local home chefs, easy direct contact, and flexible meal plans (no misleading numbers).
4. **Popular Cities Grid**: Quick access to 12 major Indian cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad, Nagpur, Kolkata, Jaipur, Surat, Indore) plus an All Cities modal.
5. **Explore by Preference**: 11 food & service categories (Veg, Jain, Non-Veg, Homemade, Student, Office, Monthly, Daily, Lunch, Dinner, Breakfast).
6. **How It Works**: 4-step progressive workflow from search to provider contact.
7. **Featured / Popular Providers**: Realistic sample listings with dietary badges, starting rates, delivery radius, Call, WhatsApp, and Quick View details modal.
8. **Interactive Map Preview**: Two-column layout with synchronized provider cards, pulsing map markers, and simulated road canvas (prepared for Google Maps API).
9. **Nearby Discovery**: Dedicated local intent section with explicit geolocation and friendly fallback.
10. **Customer Lead Generation**: Form with frontend validation for instant quotes and dietary specifications.
11. **Business Owner CTA & Future Premium Model**: Distinct dark-theme section highlighting benefits, listing modal, and future business tools.
12. **Accessible FAQ Accordion**: 8 essential questions with semantic structure and valid FAQPage schema.
13. **SEO Informational Content**: Well-crafted guide to Indian tiffin services, hygiene, and provider independence.
14. **Browse by State**: 16 Indian states/UTs plus modal with all 36 Indian states and territories.
15. **Final Customer CTA**: Dual-path conversion for hungry customers and kitchen owners.
16. **Multi-Column Footer**: Quick links, legal disclaimers, and social channels.
17. **SEO & Structured Data**: Valid JSON-LD schemas for `WebSite`, `Organization`, `WebPage`, and `FAQPage`.
